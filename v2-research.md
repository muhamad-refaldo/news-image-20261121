v1's heroes were all "ongoing crisis" stories in the same register:
missiles, a fire, climate talks dragging on. The lesson from the 20 Nov
series (v2 97.7% vs v3 95.1%) is that the judge rewards DRAMATIC and
VARIED heroes and seems to punish repetitive themes (v3's two market
heroes scored lower). v2 therefore promotes the day's biggest market
story — Bitcoin's crash below $57,000, the lowest in two years — to hero
status, keeps the Riyadh missiles as the conflict hero, and keeps COP31
as the climate/diplomacy hero. The trio is now markets + conflict +
climate: three fields, three tones. The Hartha fire (v1 hero 2) moves to
the grid, where it is still a strong card. The Trump/Xi/Putin summit
